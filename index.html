01 About background & context

I'm a data science graduate student based in Berlin, currently completing my M.Sc. in Data Science at the University of Europe for Applied Sciences (Potsdam), supervised by Dr. Humera Noor Minhas. My work centers on turning messy, real-world data — race telemetry, IoT sensor streams, social text — into models people can actually act on.

My capstone, PitWall Intelligence, predicts Formula 1 constructor performance and sponsorship value from two decades of race data, validated to a test R² of 0.976. In parallel, I've worked on anomaly detection for smart buildings, sentiment analysis at scale, and applied ETL/dashboard projects.

Before this, I completed a B.E. in Computer Engineering (Honours in Data Science) at the University of Mumbai, alongside the McKinsey Forward Program and case simulations with Accenture, BCG X, and Tata. I'm now looking for a Werkstudent role in data or analytics to build alongside my degree.

02 Projects selected work
P.01
Capstone
PitWall Intelligence

Predictive modeling of Formula 1 constructor performance and sponsorship value across the 2014–2025 V6 hybrid era, using 112 team-seasons of race data from the Jolpica-F1 API. Includes a gradient-boosted points predictor, a podium-probability classifier, SHAP-based interpretability, and a custom two-dimensional Brand Value Index.

Smart Building Anomaly Detection

Multi-model anomaly detection pipeline for building energy and IoT sensor data, combining the ASHRAE Great Energy Predictor III dataset (565 buildings, ~4.9M rows) with a live 2024 Sharjah IoT stream. Uses statistical baselines, Isolation Forest, and an LSTM autoencoder, with an LLM layer for anomaly explanation.
