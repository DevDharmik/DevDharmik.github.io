CAPSTONE · MOTORSPORT ANALYTICS

PitWall Intelligence

A stakeholder-facing dashboard that turns 11 seasons of F1 telemetry (2014–2024, 112 constructor-seasons) into a two-dimensional Brand Value Index — Performance (60%) plus Consistency (40%) — for a sponsorship market worth roughly $1.8B a year that's still priced mostly on perception.

The finding that held up across every sprint: qualifying gap-to-pole correlates with end-of-season points at r ≈ −0.79. The BVI tracks the official standings (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.72) but isn't a copy of them — 2018 Williams finished 10th on points but ranked 3rd on the index, and McLaren's 2024 resurgence scored 85.6/100, which the model caught before the headlines did.

RESEARCH · IOT ANOMALY DETECTION

Smart Building Energy Anomaly Detection

A consensus anomaly pipeline (Z-score, IQR, Isolation Forest — flagged when 2 of 3 agree) with an LLM layer (Groq Llama 3.3 70B) that turns each flag into a plain-language explanation a facilities manager can act on, not a raw score.

Runs across two structurally different datasets — ASHRAE GEPIII's aggregated hourly meters and the University of Sharjah's high-frequency multi-sensor IoT feed — toggled live in the same dashboard. Making the ingestion logic dataset-agnostic rather than hardcoded to one schema was the actual engineering problem here, not the anomaly detection itself.

NLP · SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

YouTube Comment Sentiment Analysis

A 2023 undergrad paper claimed 91% F1-score for an SVM sentiment classifier — without saying on what test set, against what baseline, or where it breaks. Rebuilt from scratch to answer that honestly: 4 models running in parallel (3 classical TF-IDF baselines + a RoBERTa transformer fine-tuned on 124M tweets) on live comments pulled from any YouTube video.

Classical models trained on movie-review data break on YouTube comments — the dashboard surfaces that domain mismatch instead of hiding it. The transformer wins on accuracy; the classical models still give exact token-level attribution the transformer can't match without approximation.
